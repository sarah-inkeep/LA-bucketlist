# Hollywood Bowl

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Summer concert tradition — picnic on the bench seats while the LA Philharmonic or a headliner plays.

## The vibe

Festive and uniquely LA. Tailgating in the parking lots, wine in plastic cups, and acoustics that carry across the canyon. Dress code is casual.

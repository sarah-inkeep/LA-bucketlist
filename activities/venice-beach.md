# Venice Beach Boardwalk

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Pure LA spectacle — people-watching, beach time, and weirdness on the boardwalk.

## The vibe

Loud, colorful, and unfiltered. Street vendors, skaters, musicians, and tourists. The beach itself is calmer than the boardwalk. Very different from Malibu's quiet coves.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Venice
- **Best for:** First-time LA visitors, people-watching, beach day
- **Time needed:** Half day
- **Cost:** Free boardwalk; bike rentals extra
- **Parking / transit:** Paid lots; Expo Line to Downtown Santa Monica + bus (TODO: needs source)

## When to go

Weekday mornings for calmer beach. Summer weekends are packed. Sunset on the pier nearby.

- Walk north to Venice Canals for a quieter contrast (TODO: needs source)

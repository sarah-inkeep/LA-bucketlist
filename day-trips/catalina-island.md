# Catalina Island

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Leave LA on a ferry, spend the day in a car-free island village — golf carts, snorkeling, and beach clubs without leaving SoCal.

## The vibe

Small-town island escape. Avalon is walkable and tourist-friendly — colorful buildings, glass-bottom boats, and a relaxed vacation feel. Much quieter than mainland LA. Can feel crowded on summer weekends when ferries are full.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Avalon (main town) and Two Harbors — 22 miles off the Southern California coast [source](../external-sources/catalina-express.md)
- **Best for:** Couples, families, snorkeling, a "mini vacation" without a long drive
- **Time needed:** Full day (first ferry out, last ferry back) or overnight for a slower pace [source](../external-sources/catalina-express.md)
- **Cost:** Ferry tickets + activities; golf cart rentals, zip line, etc. extra (TODO: needs source for current fares)
- **Parking / transit:** Drive to Long Beach, San Pedro, or Dana Point ferry terminals; park at terminal lots [source](../external-sources/catalina-express.md)

## When to go

Year-round ferry service [source](../external-sources/catalina-express.md). Weekday trips mean shorter lines. Summer and holidays book up — reserve ferries early. Spring and fall offer mild weather with fewer crowds.

## Tips

- Book Catalina Express (or similar) round-trip tickets in advance [source](../external-sources/catalina-express.md)
- Rent a golf cart to explore beyond Avalon (TODO: needs source)
- Descanso Beach Club for cabanas and beachfront dining [source](../external-sources/visit-catalina-island.md)

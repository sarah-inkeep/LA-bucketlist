# Point Dume State Beach

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Dramatic Malibu coastline with a short bluff trail and whale-watching views — close enough for a half-day trip.

## The vibe

Classic SoCal coastal — surfers below, hikers on the bluff, dolphins offshore in winter. Small and scenic rather than sprawling. Parking fills up on sunny weekends.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Malibu, western LA County (~45 min from Westside)
- **Best for:** Easy hike, beach time, sunset, out-of-town guests
- **Time needed:** Half day to full day
- **Cost:** State park parking fee (TODO: needs source for current fee)
- **Parking / transit:** Car required; limited lot at Point Dume

## When to go

Weekday mornings for easier parking. Gray whale season roughly December–April (TODO: needs source).

## Tips

- Walk the bluff trail for panoramic ocean views
- Combine with lunch on Pacific Coast Highway in Malibu
- Bring layers — coastal fog is common

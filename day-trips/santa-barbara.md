# Santa Barbara

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> The "American Riviera" — a relaxed coastal town perfect for brunch, beach time, and window shopping.

## The vibe

Laid-back, sunny, and walkable downtown. Red-tile roofs, palm-lined streets, and a mix of college-town energy and resort-town polish. Feels a world away from LA traffic.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Santa Barbara County (~95 miles north on US-101)
- **Best for:** Couples, visiting family, beach + food day
- **Time needed:** Full day
- **Cost:** Free to walk; parking downtown; beach fees vary
- **Parking / transit:** Amtrak Pacific Surfliner from LA Union Station (TODO: needs source for schedule)

## When to go

Year-round mild climate. Weekdays are easier for parking. Summer weekends get busy on the waterfront.

## Tips

- Stearns Wharf and the Funk Zone are easy first-time anchors
- Stop at a roadside strawberry stand on the 101 in season
- Pair with a quick visit to nearby wine country if you leave early

10.1 mile round-trip hike on the East Fork of the San Gabriel River to a 120-foot Depression-era bridge in Sheep Mountain Wilderness. Multiple river crossings, ~809 ft elevation gain, 7+ hours typical. Trailhead: Camp Bonita Road, Azusa. Free wilderness permit required; obtain at trailhead kiosk or ranger station.

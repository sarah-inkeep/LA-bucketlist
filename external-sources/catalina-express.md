Catalina Island is about an hour by ferry from Southern California. Year-round boat transportation with up to 30 daily departures from San Pedro, Long Beach, and Dana Point. Catalina is 22 miles off the coast. Destinations include Avalon and Two Harbors. Day trips and overnight stays available.

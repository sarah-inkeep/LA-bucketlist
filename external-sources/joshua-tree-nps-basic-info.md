Official NPS basic information: Joshua Tree is open 24/7/365. No gas, restaurants, grocery stores, or hotels inside the park — bring food, water, and supplies. No interior water. Limited cell reception. Spring and holidays are busiest. Extreme summer heat; winter can be cold. Fees and passes apply at entrance.

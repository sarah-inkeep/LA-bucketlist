Sandstone Peak is the highest point in the Santa Monica Mountains at 3,111 feet. Mishe Mokwa Trailhead: 1.75 miles north of Circle X Ranch on Yerba Buena Road, Malibu. Circle X Ranch: 12896 Yerba Buena Road. Dogs on leash allowed.

Solstice Canyon offers easy, moderate, or strenuous hikes through coastal sage scrub and riparian communities. Features Keller House ruins, Roberts Ranch/Tropical Terrace ruins (Paul Revere Williams), and a perennial waterfall. Corral Canyon Road & Solstice Canyon Road, Malibu. Limited parking — carpooling encouraged on weekends.

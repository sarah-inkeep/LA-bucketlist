# Guelaguetza

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> LA's benchmark for Oaxacan food — order a mole sampler and don't skip the memelas.

## The vibe

Festive, family-run, and always buzzing. Colorful decor, live music some nights, and a crowd that knows what they're ordering. A destination, not a quick bite.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Koreatown (Olympic Blvd)
- **Best for:** Special dinners, introducing friends to Oaxacan cuisine
- **Time needed:** 1.5–2 hours
- **Cost:** \$$$–$$$$

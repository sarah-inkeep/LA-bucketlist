# Republique

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Stunning dining room, legendary brunch pastries, and a bakery case you'll want to raid.

## The vibe

Grand and bustling — high ceilings, tile floors, and a see-and-be-seen energy at brunch. Bakery counter is casual; dining room is sit-down. Always a wait at peak hours.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Hancock Park / La Brea corridor
- **Best for:** Brunch dates, pastry runs, special occasions
- **Time needed:** 1.5–2 hours sit-down; 15 min bakery
- **Cost:** $$$ brunch; bakery items less
- **Parking / transit:** Valet or street parking; near LACMA

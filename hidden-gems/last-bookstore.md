# The Last Bookstore

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: One of the largest used bookstores in the world, with surreal book tunnels perfect for an hour of wandering.

## The vibe

Eclectic, creative, and very Instagram-famous — but still a real bookstore. Cashiers, vinyl section, and local art mixed with towering shelves. Busy on weekends.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Downtown LA (Spring Street)
- **Best for:** Book lovers, rainy days, unique LA photos
- **Time needed:** 1–2 hours
- **Cost:** Free to browse; books priced individually

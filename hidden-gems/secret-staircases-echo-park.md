# Secret Stairs — Echo Park & Silver Lake

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Discover LA's network of public staircases — a free urban hike through hidden neighborhood paths.

## The vibe

Neighborhood secret that locals use for fitness walks. Mosaic steps, canyon views, and mid-century houses. Self-guided and low-key — not a formal trail system.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Echo Park, Silver Lake, Los Feliz
- **Best for:** Walkers, architecture lovers, off-the-beaten-path exploring
- **Time needed:** 1–3 hours depending on route
- **Cost:** Free

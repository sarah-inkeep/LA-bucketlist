# Bridge to Nowhere

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> A full-day adventure — river crossings, canyon scenery, and a surreal 120-foot bridge in the middle of nowhere. Nothing like the crowded Hollywood Hills trails.

## The vibe

Remote and rugged. You're walking up a wild river canyon past old road remnants to a bridge that was left standing after floods washed everything else away. Weekday hikers spread out; weekends bring bungee-jump groups (skip early Sat/Sun mornings if you want solitude). Feels like a real backcountry trip without leaving LA County.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Azusa / San Gabriel Mountains National Monument
- **Best for:** Adventurous hikers, strong fitness, summer river wading
- **Time needed:** Full day — ~10 miles round trip, 7+ hours [source](../external-sources/bridge-to-nowhere-hike.md)
- **Cost:** Free wilderness permit; Adventure Pass may be required for parking (TODO: needs source for current parking fee policy)

## When to go

Spring for moderate temps and water flow. Summer is popular for swimming holes but hot. Avoid after heavy rain — river crossings can be dangerous (TODO: needs source).

## Tips

- Get a free wilderness permit at the trailhead kiosk before you start [source](../external-sources/bridge-to-nowhere-hike.md)
- Wear shoes you don't mind getting wet — multiple river crossings
- Start early; parking lot fills on weekends
- Pack lunch — you'll want to linger at the bridge

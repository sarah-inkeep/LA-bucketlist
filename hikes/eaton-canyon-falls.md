# Eaton Canyon Falls

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Accessible foothill hike with a waterfall payoff — great for beginners and kids when water is flowing.

## The vibe

Popular and well-trafficked. Nature center at the trailhead, mostly flat until the final rocky section. Can be crowded on spring weekends after rain.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Pasadena / Altadena, San Gabriel foothills
- **Best for:** Families, beginner hikers, post-rain waterfall chasers
- **Time needed:** 2–3 hours round trip

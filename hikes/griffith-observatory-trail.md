# Griffith Observatory Trail

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Walk up to the observatory through Griffith Park for the full LA panorama — free museum at the top.

## The vibe

Classic LA outing — families, tourists, and locals sharing the trail. The observatory lawn at sunset is the payoff. Multiple route options from easy to moderate.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Griffith Park, Los Feliz
- **Best for:** Sunset views, out-of-town guests, astronomy fans
- **Time needed:** 1.5–3 hours round trip
- **Cost:** Free hike; observatory admission free (TODO: needs source)
- **Parking / transit:** Observatory lot fills early; DASH bus available (TODO: needs source)

## When to go

Arrive before sunset on clear days — parking is the bottleneck. Weekdays easier than weekends.

# Parker Mesa via Paseo Miramar

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Steady fire-road climb to one of LA's best ocean panoramas — longer and more rewarding than Runyon, with a fraction of the crowd on weekday mornings.

## The vibe

Classic Santa Monica Mountains fire road — chaparral, ocean breeze, and a bench at the top with views from Santa Monica to the Channel Islands on clear days. More of a locals' trail than a tourist stop. No dogs allowed (TODO: needs source).

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Pacific Palisades / Topanga State Park
- **Best for:** Moderate fitness, sunset hikes, ocean views without driving to Malibu
- **Time needed:** ~2.5–3 hours; ~5.3–5.5 miles round trip (TODO: needs source)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Parking / transit:** Street parking on Paseo Miramar — limited, especially weekends (TODO: needs source)

## When to go

Sunrise or sunset for golden light. Weekday mornings for easiest parking and fewer hikers. Clear days after rain for crisp island views.

## Tips

- Trailhead at the Topanga State Park sign on Paseo Miramar (TODO: needs source)
- Steady uphill the whole way — bring water, little shade
- Can connect to Los Liones Canyon for a longer loop (TODO: needs source)

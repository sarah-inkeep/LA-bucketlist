# Runyon Canyon

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: The classic "see and be seen" LA hike — steep enough to feel like a workout, views of the city and Hollywood sign.

## The vibe

Social and sunny. Influencers, dogs, and tourists on the main loop. Can feel crowded mid-morning on weekends. No shade on the main fire road.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Hollywood Hills (Fuller Ave entrance)
- **Best for:** Quick workout, city views, dog owners
- **Time needed:** 1–1.5 hours
- **Cost:** Free
- **Parking / transit:** Street parking on Fuller; very limited (TODO: needs source)

## When to go

Sunrise or weekday mornings to avoid crowds and heat. Avoid midday summer hikes.

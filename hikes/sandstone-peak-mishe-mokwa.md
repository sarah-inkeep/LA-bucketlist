# Sandstone Peak via Mishe Mokwa

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> The classic "get above it all" LA hike — a 6-mile loop to the highest point in the Santa Monicas with far fewer people than Runyon or Griffith.

## The vibe

Wild and windswept. Chaparral, enormous rock outcrops, and wide ocean-to-valley views. You're in the only congressionally designated Wilderness in the Santa Monica Mountains [source](../external-sources/nps-sandstone-peak.md). Feels remote despite being under an hour from the Westside.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Circle X Ranch area, Malibu (Yerba Buena Rd)
- **Best for:** Strong hikers, sunrise/sunset chasers, wildflower season (spring)
- **Time needed:** 3–4 hours for the Mishe Mokwa loop to Sandstone Peak (TODO: needs source for exact mileage)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Parking / transit:** Mishe Mokwa Trailhead lot on Yerba Buena Rd, 1.75 mi north of Circle X [source](../external-sources/nps-sandstone-peak.md)

## When to go

Clear days for Channel Islands views from the summit. Start early — exposed trail gets hot. Spring wildflowers on the loop.

## Tips

- Sandstone Peak is 3,111 ft — highest in the range [source](../external-sources/nps-sandstone-peak.md)
- Dogs allowed on leash [source](../external-sources/nps-sandstone-peak.md)
- Narrow, uneven trail in sections — trekking poles help
- Combine with [Solstice Canyon](./solstice-canyon.md) for a Malibu hiking day (different trailhead)

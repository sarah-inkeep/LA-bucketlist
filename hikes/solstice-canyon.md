# Solstice Canyon

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> A lush, shady canyon in Malibu with ruins and a waterfall — longer and quieter than Griffith, especially on weekday mornings.

## The vibe

Feels like a real hike, not a social scene. Riparian shade, bird calls, and unexpected history — a stone hunting cabin and a Paul Revere Williams ranch house in ruins. Weekends fill the small parking lot; weekdays you may have the canyon mostly to yourself.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Malibu, Corral Canyon (Santa Monica Mountains NRA)
- **Best for:** Moderate hikers, history buffs, hot-day shade, dogs on leash
- **Time needed:** 2–4 hours (multiple route options from easy to strenuous) [source](../external-sources/nps-solstice-canyon.md)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Parking / transit:** Small lot at Corral Canyon Rd & Solstice Canyon Rd; carpool on weekends [source](../external-sources/nps-solstice-canyon.md)

## When to go

Year-round. Spring for wildflowers; summer mornings before heat builds. Avoid midday in summer — limited shade on the steeper Rising Sun loop.

## Tips

- Solstice Canyon Trail is paved for ~½ mile — good warm-up before branching to the waterfall [source](../external-sources/nps-solstice-canyon.md)
- Pair with a [Point Dume day trip](../day-trips/point-dume-malibu.md) for a full Malibu day
- Bring water — one fountain at the lot and one ~¼ mile up the main trail [source](../external-sources/nps-solstice-canyon.md)

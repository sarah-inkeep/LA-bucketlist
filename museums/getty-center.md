# The Getty Center

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: World-class art, stunning architecture, and one of the best free views in LA — reservation required.

## The vibe

Calm, spacious, and photogenic. European art plus modern design, cactus gardens, and city vistas. More contemplative than crowded — still popular on weekends.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Brentwood, West LA
- **Best for:** Art lovers, architecture fans, first-time LA visitors, sunset views
- **Time needed:** Half day to full day
- **Cost:** Free admission; parking fee applies [source](../external-sources/getty-center-visit.md)
- **Parking / transit:** Tram from parking structure; limited transit access

## When to go

Saturday evenings for extended hours (until 9pm) [source](../external-sources/getty-center-visit.md). Reserve timed entry in advance.

## Tips

- Book free timed tickets ahead online
- Don't miss the Central Garden and the views toward the ocean

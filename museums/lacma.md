# LACMA

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Iconic Urban Light installation out front, diverse collections, and easy Metro access on the Miracle Mile.

## The vibe

Big, sprawling campus mixing outdoor sculpture, special exhibitions, and classic collections. Busy but accessible — feels like LA's main art hub.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Miracle Mile / Mid-Wilshire
- **Best for:** Art fans, Instagram moments (Urban Light), date nights
- **Time needed:** 2–4 hours
- **Cost:** Paid admission; members free (TODO: needs source for current ticket prices)
- **Parking / transit:** D Line Wilshire/Fairfax station [source](./external-sources/lacma-visit.md)

## When to go

Friday Jazz at LACMA (Apr–Oct) and summer Latin Sounds on Saturdays [source](./external-sources/lacma-visit.md). Advance tickets recommended.

## Tips

- Reserve timed tickets online
- Food trucks on the plaza if you don't want to leave campus
- Walk to nearby Miracle Mile restaurants after

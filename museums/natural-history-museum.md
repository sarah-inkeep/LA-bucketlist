# Natural History Museum

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Classic family museum — dinosaurs, the Nature Gardens, and easy pairing with California Science Center next door.

## The vibe

Grand historic building with hands-on appeal for kids and adults. Busy on weekends and school holidays. Exposition Park has a campus feel with multiple museums.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Exposition Park, near USC
- **Best for:** Families, dinosaur fans, rainy-day outings
- **Time needed:** Half day

- Rose Garden outside is a nice picnic spot

# Arts District

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Industrial-turned-creative neighborhood — mural walks, specialty coffee, and some of LA's best restaurants.

## The vibe

Converted warehouses, street art, and a mix of artists and tech offices. Walkable core around 3rd St and Mateo. Quieter on weekdays; lively at night and weekends.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Downtown LA, east of Little Tokyo
- **Best for:** Art walks, coffee crawls, dinner destinations
- **Time needed:** Half day to full day
- **Cost:** Free to walk; dining \$$$–$$$\$$

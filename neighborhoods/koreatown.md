# Koreatown

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: LA's most energetic food neighborhood — BBQ, soondubu, noraebang, and jjimjilbang spas until late.

## The vibe

Bright signs, packed restaurants, and a city-that-never-sleeps feel. Multilingual and multicultural beyond just Korean — Central American spots mixed in. Always something open.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Mid-Wilshire, centered on Olympic Blvd
- **Best for:** Group dinners, late-night eats, spa days
- **Time needed:** Evening to late night
- **Cost:** $$ for BBQ; spas vary (TODO: needs source)
- **Parking / transit:** Wilshire/Western Metro; valet at many BBQ spots

# Silver Lake

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: LA's indie-creative hub — walk the reservoir, browse Sunset Blvd shops, grab coffee on Silver Lake Blvd.

## The vibe

Young, walkable, and dog-friendly. Mix of old LA bungalows and new development. Busy on weekends at the meadow and along Sunset Junction.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Eastside, between Echo Park and Los Feliz
- **Best for:** Afternoon wandering, casual dates, people-watching
- **Time needed:** Half day
- **Cost:** Free to walk; meals and shopping vary

# Downtown LA

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: LA's renaissance neighborhood — food halls, architecture, and culture within walking distance.

Mix of historic buildings and new towers. Busy weekday lunch crowd; quieter weekends in some blocks. Skid Row adjacent areas require awareness (TODO: needs source).

- **Neighborhood / area:** Downtown LA

Weekday lunch for Grand Central Market energy. Evening for concerts and bars.

# Griffith Park

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: The green heart of LA — enough trails, attractions, and views for dozens of repeat visits.

## The vibe

Sprawling and varied — hikers, equestrians, picnickers, and tourists at the observatory all share the same hills. Can feel wild despite being in the city.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Los Feliz / Griffith Park
- **Best for:** Hiking, LA Zoo, observatory, horseback riding
- **Time needed:** Half day to multiple days to explore fully
- **Cost:** Park free; attractions priced separately
- **Parking / transit:** Multiple lots; limited at observatory (TODO: needs source)

## When to go

Weekday mornings for trails. Observatory sunset is peak demand.

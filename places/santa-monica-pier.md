# Santa Monica Pier

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Touristy but essential — ferris wheel views, street performers, and the end of Route 66.

## The vibe

Carnival energy on the pier; beach town chill on the sand. Crowded summer weekends. Best at golden hour.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Santa Monica
- **Best for:** First-time visitors, families, sunset photos
- **Time needed:** 2–3 hours
- **Cost:** Free pier; rides and games extra

## When to go

Sunset for photos. Weekday afternoons less crowded.

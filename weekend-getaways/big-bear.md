# Big Bear Lake

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Four-season mountain town — ski in winter, lake and trails in summer, cozy cabins year-round.

## The vibe

Alpine village energy with pine trees and a walkable lakefront. Family-friendly and outdoorsy. Can feel crowded on holiday ski weekends.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** San Bernardino Mountains (~100 miles from LA)
- **Best for:** Families, ski/snow trips, lake weekends, cabin rentals
- **Time needed:** 2 nights minimum
- **Cost:** Lift tickets in winter; lake activities in summer (TODO: needs source for pricing)
- **Parking / transit:** Car required; check chain requirements in winter

## When to go

Winter for snow sports; summer for hiking and lake [source](../external-sources/visit-big-bear.md). Fall is quieter with fall color.

## Tips

- Book cabins early for holiday weekends
- Village shops and restaurants are walkable from the lake
- Check road conditions before winter drives

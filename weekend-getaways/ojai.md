# Ojai

> [!TIP] Quick take
>
> Quiet valley town for slow weekends — farm stands, spa hotels, and easy hikes without the desert heat.

## The vibe

Small, artsy, and wellness-oriented. Orange groves, independent shops, and a relaxed pace. The valley glows at sunset (locally called the "pink moment") (TODO: needs source).

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Ventura County (~90 miles from LA via US-101)
- **Best for:** Couples, reset weekends, wine and farm-to-table fans
- **Time needed:** 2 nights
- **Cost:** Mid-range inns and B&Bs; free hiking nearby
- **Parking / transit:** Car required

## When to go

Spring for wildflowers; fall for harvest events. Avoid peak fire season cautions in late summer/fall (TODO: needs source).

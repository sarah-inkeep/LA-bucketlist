# Palm Springs

> [!TIP]
> Quick take: Pool-and-cocktail desert weekend with iconic midcentury style and easy access to Joshua Tree day trips.

## The vibe

Retro-cool, sunny, and unhurried. Boutique hotels, design shops, and a strong dining scene. Quieter than LA but lively around events and snowbird season.

## Good to know

- **Neighborhood / area:** Coachella Valley (~110 miles from LA)
- **Best for:** Couples, design lovers, pool weekends, winter warm-up
- **Time needed:** 2–3 nights
- **Cost:** Hotels vary widely; many have day passes for pools (TODO: needs source)
- **Parking / transit:** Car helpful; downtown Palm Springs is walkable

## When to go

October–April for comfortable heat [source](../external-sources/visit-palm-springs.md). Summer is very hot. Events and holidays book up early.
